# Assignment brief — summary (INFS5706, T3 2026)

## The case: Cross-Sector Scam Response
Scams cross organisational boundaries (telcos, platforms, banks, government, support services). People report to whichever organisation they reach first; that organisation sees only part of the incident. In 2025 Australians made 480,000+ scam reports with losses over $2.18B (ACCC).

## The consortium: ScamSafe Alliance (fictional)
Banks/payment providers, telcos, digital platforms, government scam-reporting services, identity-recovery/victim-support orgs, community organisations. Agreed need: clearer responsibilities, consistent information, trusted referral pathways, effective coordination.

## The approved AI initiative
An AI-enabled solution supporting **authorised frontline employees** who receive suspected-scam reports. It should help staff:
1. gather and structure relevant information
2. identify missing, conflicting or uncertain information
3. recognise indicators of urgency or vulnerability
4. access approved scam-response guidance
5. support selection of an appropriate response pathway
6. prepare guidance for the affected person
7. identify when specialist or human intervention is required
8. prepare consistent case records and handovers for other organisations

Operating principle: **AI supports analysis and prepares recommendations; authorised people verify, communicate and act.**

Hard limits: the AI must **not** independently confirm a scam, block transactions, modify accounts, contact another organisation, or make consequential decisions about an affected person.

## Deliverables (team-marked)
### Part A — Presentation
6 minutes, max 6 slides. Must cover: how the solution addresses the Alliance's requirements; what it will and won't do; how it embeds within member organisations. Must include the 60–90 s prototype video. All members present; no notes/cue cards/phones (mark capped at 55% if used; Equitable Learning Plan exempt).

### Part B — Prototype
Functional prototype (chatbot, agent, vibe-coded app, workflow, hybrid…). **Must actually work**: take in a scam case, process it, produce useful output for a frontline employee. Static mock-ups, storyboards, screens or role-play are insufficient. Synthetic data only; any invented guidance must be labelled synthetic. Record a 60–90 s demo video.

### Part C — Oral defence
6 minutes immediately after. Questions may cover: robustness to change/unexpected incidents; understanding of the case; how the prototype works; why this AI approach; assumptions and limitations; human vs AI responsibilities. Same no-aids rule.
